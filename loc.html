Parish & Leadership Details

  • Patroness: Our Lady of Lourdes (Lourdu Annai).
  • Parish Priest: Fr. Satheeshkumar Joy J.
  • Community Size: Approximately 620 joint families (around 3,200 members), organized into 13 Basic Christian Communities (BCCs).
  • Campus Facilities: Main parish church, St. Lourdes Auditorium, Punnainagar R.C. Primary School and nearby St. Jude's Shrine at Punnainagar Junction.
🕊

Mass, Rosary & Novena Timings

  • Daily Services (Monday–Saturday): Rosary at 5:30 AM, followed by Morning Mass at 6:00 AM. Evening Mass is also noted at 6:00 PM.
  • Sunday Masses: Tamil Masses are listed at 6:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
  • English Mass: Listed on the parish site for the third Sunday at 10:00 AM. Some visitor reports mention Sunday English Mass at 10:30 AM.
  • Weekly Novenas: St. Antony on Tuesdays and Our Lady of Lourdes on Fridays.
  • Online Broadcasts: Watch live Tamil Masses and festival liturgies on our YouTube Channel .
📜

History & Milestones

  • Origins (1939–1946): Started as a thatched prayer cell by 13 local families, then known as Punnakattuvilai. It developed into a tile-roofed church in 1946.
  • First Mass & Consecration: The first Holy Mass was celebrated on November 22, 1953, by Rev. D.C. Antony. The present church building was consecrated on May 30, 1982.
  • Independent Parish: After serving as a substation under Kurusady and later Carmel Nagar (1970–2007), it became an independent parish on November 23, 2008, under Bishop Peter Remigius.
🤝

  • Active Ministries: Parish Council, Finance Committee, Catechetical Association, Bible Study Group, Education and Medical Committee, and Rural Women Association.
