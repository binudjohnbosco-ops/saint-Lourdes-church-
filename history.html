Our Parish Journey

A Journey Through Time

The Church of Our Lady of Lourdes in Punnai Nagar, Nagercoil, began with a small prayer cell and a tile-roofed church built by local villagers in 1946, eventually growing into an independent parish within the Diocese of Kottar.

From a humble prayer shed to an independent parish, the history of Our Lady of Lourdes Church reflects generations of faith, devotion and community unity.

1946 – The Beginning

The parish journey began with a small prayer cell and a tile-roofed church built through the efforts of local villagers.

A Growing Community

The church became a place where families gathered for prayer, worship and spiritual fellowship.

Parish Development

Through the faith and dedication of the community, the church continued to grow as an important spiritual centre.

Independent Parish

The church eventually grew into an independent parish within the Diocese of Kottar.
