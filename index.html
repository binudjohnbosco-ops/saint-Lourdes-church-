Welcome to Our Parish

The Church of Our Lady of Lourdes, Punnai Nagar, is a Roman Catholic parish church in Nagercoil, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, under the Diocese of Kottar. Our church is a place of prayer, devotion, spiritual growth and unity. We welcome all faithful and visitors to experience the love of God and the blessings of Mother Mary.
